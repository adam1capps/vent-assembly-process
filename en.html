Work Instructions

ReDry 2-Way Vent — Assembly & Packing

For Abby & Alberto · August 23, 2026 · Photos from the shop walkthrough, Aug 22, 2026

What you are building

The ReDry 2-Way Vent: a roof-drying vent built on a spun-aluminum base. The white two-way vent body (with the fan inside) locks onto the aluminum standpipe, a white sleeve and elbow hood cover it, and the finished vents are packed two per box — laid head to tail — with a trim ring and an installation sheet.

Each finished box contains:

  1. Two fully assembled vents (aluminum base + vent body + sleeve + elbow hood), laid head to tail
  2. One white ReDry trim ring
  3. One printed “Vent Installation Detail” sheet
  4. Kraft paper void fill

Parts and where they live

  • Aluminum base (12" flange + standpipe) — bench / incoming rack
  • White temporary cap — parts rack
  • Two-way vent body (bell + fan box, wired) — parts rack
  • White sleeve + elbow hood — racks by the bench (hoods have red adhesive strips)
  • Long threaded screw + wing nut — hardware at the bench
  • Self-tapping button-head screws — hardware at the bench
  • ReDry trim rings — stack at the shipping cart
  • Vent Installation Detail sheets — stack at the shipping desk
  • ULINE 24×24×12 boxes (S-4910), flat — pallet by the conveyor
  • Kraft void-fill paper — dispenser under the shipping cart
  • Water-activated tape (plain + ReDry print) — tape machine on the shipping cart

Separate job — cap the bases (about 100)

A batch of about 100 bases gets the white temporary cap only: press the cap onto the standpipe and drive its screw. That is the whole job — no vent body, sleeve, or hood.

The cap is not used when assembling full vents.

Part 1 — Assemble the vent

1Start with the aluminum base

Set the base on the bench. Note the slots near the top of the standpipe — the vent body locks through these. No cap on these bases.

2Know the vent body

The white two-way vent body: round bell that fits the standpipe, square fan box with the round port, and the fan lead (red/black wires with the connector). There are pass-through holes at the neck — find them before you start.

3Set the vent body on the standpipe and drop in the long screw

Seat the bell over the standpipe. Drop the long threaded screw down through the neck hole so it passes through the standpipe slot.

4Wing nut from below — snug it

Thread the wing nut onto the screw from underneath and tighten until the body is locked to the standpipe. Snug is enough — do not crush the plastic. Keep the fan lead tucked so it never gets pinched.

5Slide the white sleeve over the standpipe

Bring over the sleeve and the elbow hood. Slide the sleeve down over the standpipe and line up its pre-drilled hole with the aluminum.

6Screw the sleeve to the standpipe

Drive a self-tapping button screw through the pre-drilled hole so it bites the aluminum.

7Set the elbow hood and screw it at the seam

Set the elbow hood over the sleeve, opening facing down. Drive self-tapping screws at the hood-to-sleeve seam (and the second seam screw if the unit calls for it).

Assembly check: body locked (wing nut snug), both seam screws in, fan lead and connector protected inside, nothing rattles.

Part 2 — Box it

8Fold a box

Take a flat 24×24×12 blank from the pallet and fold it on the conveyor table.

9Tape the bottom — H pattern

At the tape machine: set the length on the dial, press the lever, and it feeds wetted gummed tape. One strip down the center seam first, then the edge strips to finish the H. Keep the machine's water tank filled so the tape sticks.

10Two vents go in — head to tail

Flip the box upright. Each box gets two vents, laid head to tail — the second turned opposite to the first, so each hood sits beside the other vent's flange.

11Pack it with kraft paper

Pull kraft paper from the dispenser, crumple it, and pack it around the vent so nothing shifts.

12Add the installation sheet and the trim ring

Every box gets one Vent Installation Detail sheet and one ReDry trim ring, on top of the packed vent.

13Close and seal with ReDry tape

Fold the flaps and seal the top with the ReDry-printed gummed tape strip.
