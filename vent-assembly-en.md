## What you are building

The **ReDry 2-Way Vent**: a roof-drying vent built on a spun-aluminum base. The white two-way vent body (with the fan inside) locks onto the aluminum standpipe, a white sleeve and elbow hood cover it, and the finished vents are packed two per box — laid head to tail — with a trim ring and an installation sheet.

<img src="images/img_0099.jpg" width="440" alt="Vent body sitting on the aluminum base, elbow hood behind">

**Each finished box contains:**
1. Two fully assembled vents (aluminum base + vent body + sleeve + elbow hood), laid head to tail
2. One white ReDry trim ring
3. One printed "Vent Installation Detail" sheet
4. Kraft paper void fill

## Separate job — cap the bases (about 100)

A batch of about 100 bases gets the **white temporary cap** only: press the cap onto the standpipe and drive its screw. That is the whole job — no vent body, sleeve, or hood.

The cap is **not** used when assembling full vents.

<img src="images/img_0097.jpg" width="440" alt="White temporary cap on the standpipe">

## Part 1 — Assemble the vent

### 1. Start with the aluminum base
Set the base on the bench. Note the **slots near the top of the standpipe** — the vent body locks through these. No cap on these bases.

<img src="images/img_0098.jpg" width="440" alt="Aluminum base with slotted standpipe">

### 2. Know the vent body
The white two-way vent body: round bell that fits the standpipe, square fan box with the round port, and the fan lead (red/black wires with the connector). There are **pass-through holes at the neck** — find them before you start.

<img src="images/img_0100.jpg" width="330" alt="Pointing out the hole at the neck"> <img src="images/img_0101.jpg" width="330" alt="Second hole location on the neck">

### 3. Set the vent body on the standpipe and drop in the long screw
Seat the bell over the standpipe. Drop the **long threaded screw** down through the neck hole so it passes through the standpipe slot.

<img src="images/img_0102.jpg" width="330" alt="Long threaded screw going into the neck hole"> <img src="images/img_0103.jpg" width="330" alt="Screw dropped through the neck">

### 4. Wing nut from below — snug it
Thread the **wing nut** onto the screw from underneath and tighten until the body is locked to the standpipe. Snug is enough — do not crush the plastic. Keep the fan lead tucked so it never gets pinched.

<img src="images/img_0104.jpg" width="440" alt="Wing nut threaded onto the screw under the neck">

### 5. Slide the white sleeve over the standpipe
Bring over the sleeve and the elbow hood. Slide the sleeve down over the standpipe and line up its pre-drilled hole with the aluminum.

<img src="images/img_0105.jpg" width="440" alt="Base, sleeve and elbow hood staged on the bench">

### 6. Screw the sleeve to the standpipe
Drive a **self-tapping button screw** through the pre-drilled hole so it bites the aluminum.

<img src="images/img_0108.jpg" width="330" alt="Screw lined up with the pre-drilled hole"> <img src="images/img_0109.jpg" width="330" alt="Screw driven at the sleeve-to-standpipe seam">

### 7. Set the elbow hood and screw it at the seam
Set the elbow hood over the sleeve, opening facing down. Drive self-tapping screws at the hood-to-sleeve seam (and the second seam screw if the unit calls for it).

<img src="images/img_0110.jpg" width="330" alt="Screw at the hood-to-sleeve seam"> <img src="images/img_0112.jpg" width="330" alt="Screw offered up at the lower seam">

**Assembly check:** body locked (wing nut snug), both seam screws in, fan lead and connector protected inside, nothing rattles.

## Part 2 — Box it

### 8. Fold a box
Take a flat **24×24×12** blank from the pallet and fold it on the conveyor table.

<img src="images/img_0113.jpg" width="330" alt="Pallet of flat box blanks"> <img src="images/img_0115.jpg" width="330" alt="Folding the box">

### 9. Tape the bottom — H pattern
At the tape machine: set the length on the dial, press the lever, and it feeds wetted gummed tape. One strip down the center seam first, then the edge strips to finish the **H**.

<img src="images/img_0117.jpg" width="220" alt="Length dial on the tape machine"> <img src="images/img_0118.jpg" width="220" alt="Pressing the lever to feed tape"> <img src="images/img_0119.jpg" width="220" alt="Center seam taped"> <img src="images/img_0123.jpg" width="220" alt="Finished H-pattern seal">

Keep the machine's water tank filled so the tape sticks.

### 10. Two vents go in — head to tail
Flip the box upright. **Each box gets two vents, laid head to tail** — the second turned opposite to the first, so each hood sits beside the other vent's flange.

<img src="images/img_0124.jpg" width="330" alt="Vent placed in the box hood-down"> <img src="images/img_0125.jpg" width="330" alt="Vent seated in the box, flange up">

### 11. Pack it with kraft paper
Pull kraft paper from the dispenser, crumple it, and pack it around the vent so nothing shifts.

<img src="images/img_0127.jpg" width="330" alt="Pulling kraft paper from the dispenser"> <img src="images/img_0128.jpg" width="330" alt="Vent packed with kraft paper">

### 12. Add the installation sheet and the trim ring
Every box gets **one Vent Installation Detail sheet** and **one ReDry trim ring**, on top of the packed vent.

<img src="images/img_0132.jpg" width="330" alt="Vent Installation Detail sheet"> <img src="images/img_0134.jpg" width="330" alt="ReDry trim ring going into the box">

<img src="images/img_0135.jpg" width="440" alt="Packed box: vent, ring, and folded sheet inside">

### 13. Close and seal with ReDry tape
Fold the flaps and seal the top with the **ReDry-printed gummed tape** strip.

<img src="images/img_0129.jpg" width="330" alt="Closing the box flaps"> <img src="images/img_0130.jpg" width="330" alt="ReDry-branded tape strip across the top seam">

## Final check before it leaves your station

- [ ] Wing nut snug — vent body does not twist on the standpipe
- [ ] Seam screws in at sleeve and hood
- [ ] Fan lead and connector tucked and unpinched
- [ ] Box bottom sealed in an H pattern
- [ ] Contents: two vents head to tail + trim ring + installation sheet + void fill
- [ ] Top sealed with ReDry tape; FRAGILE stickers from the cart if required
